Engineering

Rensch Engineering provides engineering consulting and design services for commercial, industrial, and residential building systems. These systems include heating, ventilation, air conditioning, plumbing, power, lighting, and process cooling and heating systems. We provide services to a broad range of clients, including architects, design-build contractors, facility managers, developers, and owners. Our full MEP (mechanical, electrical, and plumbing) design and consulting services can be tailored to meet your specific project requirements.

New Building Projects:

With over a decade of experience designing more than 1,500 projects, our expert services are vital to your building design.

Renovation Building Projects

For renovations, MEP systems need to complement each other, sometimes requiring integration with existing systems. Our full MEP experience will efficiently bring your project to fruition by handling these interconnected systems all in one place.

Performance Specifications:

When design budgets are tight, performance specifications can be a good solution to establishing good engineering design performance for contractor design-build work.

Existing Building MEP Surveys:

Our survey team has surveyed hundreds of locations which brings a wealth of knowledge to existing building and due diligence surveys.

Sustainable Design and LEED Assessment:

We have multiple LEED-certified engineers with practical design experience on LEED projects to meet your sustainable energy needs.

Energy Code Conformance:

The complexities of energy codes are ever-growing, but we help you meet those requirements, from lighting controls to heat pump water heaters.

Lighting Design and Footcandle Calculations:

From parking lots to interior lighting, we select fixtures and provide footcandle calculations to meet city ordinances and owner requirements. We understand the aesthetic needs of a project and will work with you to find the fixture you want while bringing our expertise to ensure it will perform.

Consolidated MEP

Rensch Engineering has been providing mechanical, electrical, and plumbing engineering in one office since it was established in 2009. Having all three of these disciplines in one office allows for easier coordination with the overall design team. There is also a synergy that happens when all three engineering disciplines are in one office together where inventive ideas are developed.
