About Us

Rensch Engineering was founded by Christopher Rensch, PE. in 2009. Rensch Engineering Team has experience in multiple types of mechanical, electrical, and plumbing projects and has successfully completed more than 1,500 projects nationwide. Rensch engineers are committed to the projects they design and look forward to working on yours.

Rensch Engineering is also listed as a Veteran Owned Business.

Rensch Engineering is currently registered in the following states

Washington | Oregon | Montana | California | Nevada | Idaho | Alaska | Utah | Texas | Missouri | Minnesota | Colorado | New Mexico
